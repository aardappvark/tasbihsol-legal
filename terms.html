3. Description of Service

TasbihSol is a digital Islamic tasbih (prayer bead counter) companion application designed for the Solana Seeker device. The App provides:

  • A digital misbaha (prayer bead) interface for counting dhikr
  • Pre-configured dhikr sets including Standard Tasbih, 99 Names of Allah, Morning Adhkar, and Evening Adhkar
  • A free count mode for personal du'as and custom recitations
  • Arabic text with English transliteration and translation
  • Hadith quotes from authentic collections
  • Prayer tracking, statistics, and streak monitoring
  • Home screen widgets for quick dhikr access
  • Daily dhikr reminder notifications
TasbihSol is NOT a financial service. The App does not facilitate, enable, or support any financial transactions, token transfers, cryptocurrency trading, DeFi protocols, staking, lending, borrowing, or any other financial activity. Wallet connection via Sign In With Solana (SIWS) is used exclusively for identity verification and Seeker Genesis Token (SGT) ownership verification.
