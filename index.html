TasbihSol

Digital Islamic Tasbih for Solana Seeker

Built for Solana Seeker

TasbihSol is a comprehensive digital tasbih (prayer bead counter) application designed exclusively for the Solana Seeker device. Practice your daily dhikr with beautiful Arabic calligraphy, track your devotional progress, and deepen your connection through remembrance of Allah.

Features

Digital Misbaha

A beautiful 99-bead tasbih visualization with haptic feedback on each count. Swipe or tap to progress through your dhikr with an authentic prayer bead experience.

5 Dhikr Sets

Standard Tasbih (SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar), 99 Names of Allah, Morning Adhkar, Evening Adhkar, and Free Count mode for personal du'as.

Arabic + English

Full Arabic text displayed alongside English transliteration and translation. Read the original script and understand every phrase you recite.

Hadith Wisdom

Daily quotes from authentic Hadith collections including Sahih Bukhari and Sahih Muslim. Learn the virtues behind each dhikr you perform.

Prayer Tracking

Statistics, daily streaks, and favorites to keep you motivated. Track your devotional consistency and set personal dhikr goals.

Home Screen Widgets

Quick-access widgets for your most-used dhikr sets. Start counting directly from your home screen without opening the app.
