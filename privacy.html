1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TasbihSol ("the App", "we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our digital Islamic tasbih application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

TasbihSol is a devotional tool designed to assist Muslims in performing dhikr (remembrance of Allah). The App is designed with a privacy-first architecture: we do not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal information whatsoever.

3. Data We Collect

We collect NO personal data.

TasbihSol does not collect, transmit, or process any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information (PII)
  • User accounts or registration data
  • Analytics or usage telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking pixels

We do not use any third-party analytics services, advertising SDKs, crash reporting tools, or data collection frameworks.

4. Local Storage

TasbihSol stores the following data exclusively on your device using Android's local storage mechanisms (SharedPreferences and encrypted storage). This data is never transmitted to any server:

Data Purpose Storage Location
Dhikr preferences Remember your preferred dhikr sets, count targets, and display settings Device only
Tasbih statistics Track your daily counts, streaks, and completion history Device only
Favourites Store your bookmarked du'as, adhkar, and Names of Allah Device only
Notification preferences Daily dhikr reminder times and enabled/disabled state Device only
Wallet public address Solana wallet address used for Sign In With Solana (SIWS) identity verification Encrypted on device only
Region screening result Boolean flag indicating whether the user's region is permitted Device only

All locally stored data is deleted when you uninstall the App. You may also clear this data at any time through your device's application settings.

5. Network Requests

TasbihSol makes minimal network requests, limited to the following:

Request Destination Purpose Data Sent
SGT Verification Solana RPC (mainnet-beta) Verify Seeker Genesis Token ownership for dApp Store compliance Wallet public address only

No user data, usage patterns, dhikr statistics, preferences, or any other information is transmitted to any server. The SGT verification query sends only the wallet's public address (which is already publicly visible on the Solana blockchain) to the Solana RPC endpoint.

6. Permissions Explained

TasbihSol requests the following Android permissions, each with a specific and limited purpose:

Permission Purpose Data Impact
INTERNET Required for Solana RPC communication to verify SGT ownership Only wallet public address is sent; no personal data transmitted
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Region verification for sanctions compliance screening Processed entirely on-device. No location data is transmitted, stored, or logged. Used only to determine MCC/region code.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Reschedule daily dhikr reminder notifications after device restart No data collected or transmitted
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Display daily dhikr reminders at user-configured times No data collected or transmitted
VIBRATE Haptic feedback when counting tasbih beads No data collected or transmitted

7. No Automated Decision-Making

TasbihSol does not use artificial intelligence, machine learning, profiling, or any form of automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on users.

The sanctions/region screening feature is a deterministic, rule-based check that compares device locale and network operator information against a static list of restricted jurisdictions. This check is performed entirely on-device and does not involve any external processing or profiling.

8. Children's Privacy

TasbihSol does not collect personal data from any user, regardless of age. The App does not contain any mechanism for data collection, account creation, or user identification. As such, no special provisions for children's data are necessary, though we note compliance with the following:

10. Data Retention

All data generated by TasbihSol is stored exclusively on your device. We do not retain any data on servers, cloud storage, or any off-device location.

  • Uninstallation: All App data is automatically deleted when you uninstall TasbihSol.
  • Manual Clearing: You can clear all App data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > TasbihSol > Storage > Clear Data.
  • No Backups: TasbihSol does not back up data to any cloud service.

11. Do Not Track

TasbihSol respects the Do Not Track (DNT) browser/device signal. However, as the App does not track users in any capacity, the DNT signal has no practical effect — tracking is never performed regardless of the signal's state.

12. Local Storage Disclosure (ePrivacy Directive)

In compliance with the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC as amended by 2009/136/EC), we disclose that TasbihSol stores data locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and EncryptedSharedPreferences. This local storage is strictly necessary for the functioning of the App (storing your dhikr preferences, statistics, and notification settings) and does not involve any tracking or profiling. No cookies or web-based storage mechanisms are used.
